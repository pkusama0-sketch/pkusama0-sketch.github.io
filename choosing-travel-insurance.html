How to compare travel insurance without getting overwhelmed

Price is only one part of travel insurance. Compare the policy wording against your destination, activities and health situation.

Begin with your trip

Write down your destinations, dates, planned activities and any expensive items you will take. Check whether a visa or airline has a minimum insurance requirement. These details help you compare policies on a like-for-like basis.

Read the medical section closely

Check emergency medical limits, hospital arrangements and the emergency assistance number. Existing medical conditions and certain activities may be excluded or need to be declared. Never rely only on a summary table.

Understand cancellation and delay cover

Look at the stated reasons for cancellation, the excess payable on a claim and evidence you may need. A policy will not normally cover every change of mind or every disruption.

Check exclusions before buying

Read exclusions relating to alcohol, unattended belongings, high-risk activities, areas under travel warnings and pre-existing conditions. Ask the insurer directly if wording is unclear, and keep their answer in writing.

Make a practical choice

Choose a regulated insurer where possible, save the policy documents and store the emergency number offline. This article is general information, not insurance advice.
