A simple checklist for choosing temporary accommodation

A cheap room is not always a good first choice. Compare the full cost, location and cancellation rules before committing.

Start with location, not photos

Check the property on a map. Is it close to a transport stop, the place you need to visit and shops for essentials? Read recent reviews for comments about noise, safety, cleanliness and check-in. One attractive photo does not show the whole experience.

Calculate the complete price

Look beyond the nightly rate. Check taxes, cleaning fees, deposits, breakfast, luggage storage and any payment due at arrival. If a host asks you to pay outside the booking platform, treat it as a warning sign unless you can independently verify the business.

Read the cancellation rules

Plans can change because of delayed flights, document problems or illness. Choose terms you understand and take a screenshot of the booking confirmation. Confirm the check-in time and what to do if you arrive late.

Keep communication on record

Use the booking platform's messages where possible. If a host changes the address, price or payment method, contact the platform's support before acting. Never share unnecessary identity documents through an unverified message.

Have a backup plan

Save two alternative stays and enough money for one night somewhere else. It is a small precaution that can make an unexpected problem much easier to manage.
