How to keep important documents organised while travelling

Keeping documents organised means you can answer questions and solve problems more quickly. Use a simple system that protects originals and makes copies easy to find.

Separate originals from copies

Keep your passport, required visa documents and payment cards close to you in a secure place. Store photocopies or digital copies separately. If a bag goes missing, having copies elsewhere can help you make a report or request assistance.

Create one protected digital folder

Save clear scans of your passport photo page, travel tickets, accommodation confirmation, insurance documents and emergency contacts. Protect the account with a strong unique password and multi-factor authentication. Avoid using a shared or public device to open the folder.

Write down key details

Keep a small paper note with emergency contacts, the address of your accommodation and important booking numbers. A paper backup is useful if your phone battery is empty or you cannot access the internet.

Check expiry dates early

Before every trip, check your passport expiry date and the validity of any visa or travel document. Some destinations have rules about how long a passport must remain valid, so check official government or embassy information for your own journey.

Know what to do if something is lost

Save the contact details for your embassy or consulate before travelling. If documents are lost or stolen, report the issue promptly to the relevant local authority and follow the official instructions for replacement.
