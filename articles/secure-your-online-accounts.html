Five small steps to secure your online accounts

You do not need to do everything at once. Start with email, banking and the accounts that could reset your other passwords.

1. Use a unique password for every important account

A password manager can create and remember long, random passwords. Avoid reusing a password: one breach should not unlock several accounts.

2. Turn on multi-factor authentication

Use an authenticator app, security key or another method offered by the service. Save backup codes somewhere secure, not in an unprotected notes app.

3. Protect your email first

Email is often the path to password resets. Review its password, recovery email/phone and active sessions before moving to less important accounts.

4. Watch for convincing phishing

Do not sign in through a link in an unexpected message. Open the service yourself, and check a sender address carefully. Urgency is a common warning sign.

5. Review account activity

Remove devices and apps you do not recognise. Enable security alerts, and keep your phone and computer updated.
