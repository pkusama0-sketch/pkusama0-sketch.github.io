A document checklist before moving to another country

A well-organised document folder can make a move much less stressful. Start early, and check every requirement with the destination's official authorities.

1. Start with identity documents

Check the expiry date of every passport. Keep copies of your passport's photo page, national identity document, birth certificate and any documents that show a name change. Your destination may ask for certified copies, translations or legalisation—requirements differ by country.

2. Gather your immigration records

Keep copies of your visa application, approval notice, entry requirements and residence permit once issued. Do not assume a visa is enough: some countries require registration after arrival.

3. Organise education and work records

Collect diplomas, transcripts, professional licences, employment letters and a current CV. Ask whether documents need an official translation or verification before you travel.

4. Prepare health and family documents

Depending on your circumstances, this may include vaccination records, medical summaries, marriage certificates and children's school records. Discuss prescriptions and continuity of care with a qualified clinician.

5. Keep a safe digital copy

Scan important documents, protect the folder with a strong unique password, and keep an offline backup. Digital copies are useful, but carry original documents when an authority requires them.

Before you finalise plans

Use this list as an organiser, not as a legal checklist. Visit the official immigration website or embassy for your destination, because requirements, validity periods and fees can change.
