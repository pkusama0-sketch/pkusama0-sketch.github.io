Plan a first-month budget when moving somewhere new

The first month is usually more expensive than normal life. A simple estimate helps you avoid arriving without enough money for essentials.

List one-time setup costs

Include deposits, initial rent, transport from the airport, a temporary stay, basic furniture, local SIM costs and any document fees. Confirm deposits and payment terms directly with landlords or providers before transferring money.

Estimate routine costs

Research local transport, food, utilities, phone service and essential healthcare needs. Use recent prices from trustworthy local sources and convert them with a small buffer for exchange-rate movement.

Separate required money from a buffer

Keep a realistic emergency buffer for delays, unexpected accommodation and document changes. Avoid treating borrowed money or future earnings as guaranteed.

Track the first 30 days

Write down every expense after arrival. Your real costs will quickly show what needs adjusting for the following month.

This is a planning guide, not financial advice. Consider advice from a regulated professional for major financial decisions.
